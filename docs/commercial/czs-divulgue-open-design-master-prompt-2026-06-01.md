# Prompt Mestre Open Design - Landing Comercial CZS

## Objetivo

Refazer a landing comercial do Catálogo CZS como uma vitrine premium e cinematográfica para clientes finais de Cruzeiro do Sul, Vale do Juruá e Acre. A página deve vender presença regional conectada, não apenas um anúncio isolado.

## Prompt De Execução

Create a premium cinematic commercial landing page for Catálogo CZS, a regional digital ecosystem based in Cruzeiro do Sul, Vale do Juruá, Acre, Brazil.

The first viewport must feel like a film poster for a real local technology operation: a nighttime city avenue, strong editorial typography, deep dark-blue atmosphere, warm gold accent, subtle technical grid, and an elegant orbital distribution map connecting Catálogo CZS to Instagram, Google, WhatsApp, Facebook, the regional catalog, and the local news operation.

Position the product as a local discovery and conversion system:

- not just one ad;
- a business presence connected to regional news, catalog pages, Google search, Instagram stories, WhatsApp status and validated groups, Facebook pages, groups and Marketplace;
- a clear path from attention to discovery, discovery to contact, and contact to sale.

Use the following verified local project inventory as commercial proof. Present it honestly as produced structure and operational capacity, not invented public audience:

- 480 archived local news items;
- 81 cataloged local contacts;
- 26 business and service categories;
- 12 service modules;
- 40 publishable HTML pages;
- 111 API routes;
- 11 organized commercial campaign pieces.

Add a channel-report section using verified local logs:

- Catálogo CZS distribution command: 1610 news items in the propaganda/news engine snapshot, 12 premium queue rows, 11 WhatsApp assets, 11 story/status videos with audio, 5 news-distribution signals for WhatsApp/Instagram, and 8 sales/service WhatsApp distribution signals;
- WhatsApp: 24 operational distribution signals combining 11 prepared commercial pieces, 5 news WhatsApp/Instagram signals and 8 sales/service WhatsApp signals;
- Instagram: 41 mapped items across official, recent premium and hyperlocal routine packs: 5 official items, 24 recent premium pieces and 12 routine hyperlocal items;
- Facebook: 10 commercial package items marked for Facebook destination, including 8 Marketplace-marked items, plus page and groups according to the item type.

Label these as local logs and prepared channel inventory, not public reach or sales performance.

Show launched or available products:

- Jornal CZS with regional news, sources and archive;
- Catálogo de serviços with local search, categories, phone numbers and addresses;
- PubPaid beta with Sinuca, Damas and Xadrez as skill-game community tests, without gambling and without income promises;
- Enquete Acre 2026 as a voluntary spontaneous poll, explicitly without scientific character;
- premium landing pages and local SEO;
- websites and automated chats for businesses;
- T.I. Logístico CZS support services;
- commercial campaigns with art, copy, stories, status, links and appropriate distribution.

Expose the technology in customer language:

- local SEO with title, description, canonical, sitemap and structured data;
- structured catalog data with category, phone number, address, tags and source;
- regional content engine with news, archive, services and local utility;
- multichannel distribution with Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook and public pages;
- direct conversion with CTA, WhatsApp and commercial landing pages;
- owned experiences with beta games, poll pages, verticals and campaigns.

Keep the visual language restrained and premium:

- one dominant idea per section;
- no generic SaaS dashboard mosaic;
- no fake metrics;
- no decorative numbers without source;
- no pricing invented without an approved commercial table;
- no excessive pills or cards;
- use typography, spacing, contrast and cinematic imagery before adding UI chrome;
- preserve strong mobile reading and large touch targets;
- use motion only for entrance, subtle depth, orbital movement and proof-tape continuity.
- add interactive effects such as self-writing copy, 3D tilt reports, holographic project cards, subtle 2D ticker motion and depth-based cinematic scenes.
- add global cinematic lighting: mouse-follow spotlight, sweeping light, subtle parallax depth in the hero and glowing analytics/cockpit visuals.

Close with a direct WhatsApp conversion CTA: ask the client to tell what they sell so the team can choose the best local presence path.

## Regras De Integridade

- Não apresentar os acessos locais de teste como audiência pública.
- Métricas de alcance, seguidores, vendas e conversão só entram com relatório validado de origem e período.
- PubPaid deve ser divulgado como beta de comunidade, sem promessa de dinheiro, prêmio, Pix, saque, renda ou aposta.
- Enquete Acre 2026 deve manter aviso de sondagem espontânea voluntária sem caráter científico.
- WhatsApp deve respeitar status e grupos validados; não enviar para conversas individuais por padrão.
