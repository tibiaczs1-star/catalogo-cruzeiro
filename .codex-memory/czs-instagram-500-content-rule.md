# CZS Instagram 500 Content Rule

Goal: build toward at least 500 Instagram contents across stories, feed, reels/videos and shares.

## Hard Channel Rule

- Instagram is only for news, public-interest editorial content and services.
- Never post single-object classifieds on Instagram: phones, TVs, Meta Quest, one-off products or garage-sale items.
- Object sales stay in WhatsApp sales groups and Facebook/Marketplace.

## Publishing Rhythm

- Do not dump 500 posts blindly in one burst.
- Work in validated batches:
  - 3 to 5 news/editorial stories.
  - 1 service story.
  - 1 feed post for consolidated/high-value news.
  - reels/videos only when source video or motion package exists.
- Every batch must save evidence and log.

## Narration Rule

For every video or silent image-news package, add narration text before publishing.

Voice direction:

- Portuguese Brazil.
- Female voice.
- New, firm, imperative, newsroom/journalist tone.
- Direct and useful; no joking, no sales tone in news.

Narration structure:

1. "Atenção, Cruzeiro do Sul."
2. One sentence with the fact.
3. One sentence with why it matters today.
4. Source attribution.
5. Call to follow Catálogo CZS for updates.

## Queue Mix

- `news_story`: daily local and Acre news.
- `news_feed`: consolidated/high-impact news.
- `news_video`: source video or generated video package with narration.
- `service_story`: allowed services only.
- `share_signal`: safe sharing/repost signal from local news sources.

## Fast Recovery

If token/credit is ending, preserve:

- current queue file;
- last posted index;
- evidence folder;
- next batch recommendation;
- blocked items and why.
